## Abstract
This research deals with Gaussian curvature and some of its applications. We study curves on surfaces as a basic concept to discuss the importance and role of curvature in nature. In physics, this becomes necessary in applications of general relativity, where it is sometimes desired to calculate the Gaussian curvature at a point in space from the observed geodesic path of planets or light rays. In engineering, those involved in the design of structures such as geodesic domes frequently require a practical formula for computing Gaussian curvature.

## Contents
1. **Gauss Surface** — Geometry of curves, parametrized curves, regular curves & arc length, global properties of plane curves, regular surfaces, tangent planes, first fundamental form, orientation and the Gauss map, classification of points on a surface
2. **The Curvature in Gauss Map** — Gaussian curvature, principal curvature, mean curvature
3. **Some Applications in Gaussian Curvature** — Applications to physics, calculation of Gaussian curvature from geodesic deviation, examples of applications in engineering
